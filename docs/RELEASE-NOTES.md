Grandma Linux for Fedora Kinoite, KDE Plasma 6 and Wayland.

- Curated applications, offline dictionaries, Portugal maps and sentence translation.
- Familiar desktop appearance, optional reading presets and a bilingual offline manual.
- Local backups, recovery and updates through the System menu.

Review the tested version and known limits in docs/STATUS.md before publishing this draft. Clean VM checks do not establish physical hardware support. The local backup must also be copied to another device for disk-failure recovery.
